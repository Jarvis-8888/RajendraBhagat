About The Firm

Ahmednagar–Pune's Trusted Structural Engineering Consultancy

Founded in 2019 by Mr. Rajendra Dhondiram Bhagat — a Master's in Structural Engineering holder — our firm has rapidly grown into a leading consultancy delivering comprehensive engineering solutions across the region.

We are dedicated to transforming architectural visions into solid, safe, and sustainable structural realities.

With an unwavering commitment to innovation, precision, and excellence — we specialize in comprehensive structural solutions across a wide range of project types.

Every solution is tailored, cost-optimized, and delivered on time — meeting both functional requirements and codal compliance.
